# Closed-Loop Boost Converter – Modelling, Control & Hardware Implementation

## 📌 Overview

This project focuses on the design and implementation of a **DC-DC Boost Converter**, covering the complete development process from **inductor design and converter modelling to closed-loop control and hardware implementation**.

The converter was first modelled and simulated in **MATLAB/Simulink** to study its behaviour under different output-voltage references. A closed-loop voltage-control strategy was then developed and its performance was compared with the corresponding **open-loop operation**. The final design was implemented as a **fully functional hardware prototype**.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Design and build a functional **Boost Converter hardware prototype**.
- Design the required **inductor** based on converter specifications.
- Develop the mathematical and simulation model of the converter.
- Implement **closed-loop voltage control**.
- Compare open-loop and closed-loop performance for different output-voltage references.
- Validate the converter through both **MATLAB/Simulink simulations and hardware implementation**.

## 🔧 Key Features

- Designed the boost converter **inductor and power stage**.
- Developed the converter model in **MATLAB/Simulink**.
- Implemented **closed-loop voltage control** for output-voltage regulation.
- Compared **open-loop vs. closed-loop** responses for different voltage references.
- Built and tested a **working hardware prototype**.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- MATLAB/Simulink
- Power Electronics
- DC-DC Converter Design
- Inductor Design
- Closed-Loop Control
- Hardware Implementation
- Oscilloscope & Power Supply

## 📈 Results

The project demonstrates the complete workflow of a practical power converter—from **component design and simulation to control development and hardware validation**. The closed-loop controller provides regulated output voltage and improved tracking compared with open-loop operation.
